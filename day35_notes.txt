DAY 35 - Proxy & Web ATtack Logs Analysis
- Proxy logs show outbound web activity and are critical for detecting phishhing,malware delivery, and C2. 
 - Phishing indicators: suspicious domains, shortened URLs, credential-harvesting pages. 
 - Malware delivery often appears as suspicious file downloads (.exe, .js, .zip) from unknown domains. 
 - C2 over web shows beaconing: repeated connections at fixed intervals to the same domain. 
 - web scanning appears as rapid requests to many URLs like /admin, /login, /wp-admin. 
 - Status codes matter: 404 floods = scanning, 302 chains = phishing, 403 = probing.
 -Suspicious User-Agents include curl, wget, python-requests, or missing UA strings.
